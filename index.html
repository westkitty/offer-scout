      How the score works

      Net = total pay − miles × cost per mile.

      Time = drive (route time, or miles ÷ avg mph) + minutes per store + minutes per stop.

      Score = 60% from net $/hr vs your target + 40% from gross $/mile vs your target. Hitting a target is worth 80 points, 125% of it is worth 100. Minus points for each extra store and each stop past two.

      Accept at 70+, Maybe at 50–69, Decline below 50.
